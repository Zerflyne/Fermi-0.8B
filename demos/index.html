FERMI demos

FERMI reads a state (any text or JSON) and answers typed questions with probabilities: yes/no, pick one option, or pick a level on a scale. It never writes text. Everything below runs on your machine.

🧪

Playground

Write any state, add your own questions, see the full probability distribution for each answer.
🛡️

Live moderation

Type a chat message: FERMI flags insults, threats and spam as you write, with a severity scale.
📬

Inbox triage

Eight emails, three questions each: urgency, category, needs a reply. FERMI sorts the inbox.
❓

Twenty questions

A secret card is hidden from you but not from FERMI. Ask yes/no questions, read the odds, guess.
⭕

Tic-tac-toe

Play against FERMI. Every move is a "pick one option" question; see where it wants to play.
🐍

Snake

FERMI plays snake in real time, one "which way?" question per step. Compare a bare grid with a state full of hints.
🔴

Connect four

Play against FERMI on a 7×6 board. See the probability of every column, with or without hints about each move.
🏓

Pong

FERMI holds a paddle at 60 fps, answering "up, stay or down?" as fast as it can. Play it with the mouse or a finger.
🃏

Blackjack

FERMI decides hit or stand; the textbook basic strategy keeps score of how often it agrees with the math.
✊

Rock, paper, scissors

FERMI predicts your next move from the history and plays the counter. Are you less random than you think?
