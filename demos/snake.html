Snake

FERMI plays snake in real time. Before every step it gets the board as its state and answers one "pick one option" question: which way to go. The arrows around the head show the probability it gave each direction. Switch between the three kinds of state to see how much a classifier depends on what you tell it: a bare grid is hard to read for a 2B model, plain facts about each move are easy.
