Rock, paper, scissors

Before every round FERMI reads the history of the game and answers one question: what will the opponent play next? Then it plays the move that beats its prediction. Against a truly random player nobody can do better than a third; people (and simple bots) are not random. Try a bot to see whether FERMI spots the pattern.
